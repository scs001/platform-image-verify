# 谦面市场助手

## What this is

用 AI 直接操作谦面市场与网关：浏览/检索功集与网关条目、发布新功集与新版本、上下架、切可见性、策展分类、发布前查重。装包即得 facet_market_* 与 facet_agent_* 十二个工具（凭据走壹座既有连接：registry + 谦面市场配对），配两份手册技能讲清『怎么写 manifest、怎么过发布门、怎么安全写操作』。

- Category: facade (plaza category enum v1)
- Official: yes — curated by FindData
- AI facade: yes — deployable as a conversational service

## How to obtain

Three paths, all landing the same frozen version snapshot:

1. In 壹座 (Yizuo): open the market tab, search "谦面市场助手", subscribe and install.
2. In a CLI-capable editor (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Gemini CLI, ZCode):

       npx @finddatatechnology/facet install 0RdQE184anPUaMhIvFwndA

3. From the market page: https://plaza.finddatatech.cloud/#/packs
